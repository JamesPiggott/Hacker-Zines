## 📚 What is this?

This repository serves as a **hub for publicly available hacker zines** — collections of raw technical writing, essays, exploits, philosophy and underground culture from the early days of computing up to today.

All content hosted here is:
- Publicly released by its authors or editors
- Considered abandonware / historically archived
- Preserved for educational and research purposes
- Non-commercial

---

## 🗂️ Included Zines

### ✅ Currently archived:

- 📄 **Phrack Magazine**
  - Legendary hacker journal since 1985
  - Exploits, whitepapers, and philosophy
  - → https://phrack.org

- ⌨️ **Paged Out**
  - High-quality technical zine for modern hackers
  - Reverse engineering, OS internals, bugs
  - → https://pagedout.institute

- 🌀 **tmp.out**
  - Underground programming & software internals
  - Art meets assembly
  - → https://tmpout.sh

- ☣️ **PHRACK-like / Independent Zines**
  - Community-contributed issues and discontinued releases

## ❌ What is NOT included

Some publications are intentionally omitted for legal or ethical reasons.

- 🚫 **2600 Magazine**  
  2600 is a commercial publication. Please support the authors and buy it:

## 🧠 Why this exists

Zines were the lifeblood of hacker culture before blogs and social media.

This repository is about:
- Digital archaeology
- Preserving technical writing
- Respecting the underground
- Keeping knowledge accessible
- Sharing hacker history responsibly

If a creator requests removal, it will be honored.

## 🤝 Contributing

Want to add something?

### ✅ Acceptable Contributions:
- Public domain zines
- Author-released archives
- Dead websites / lost projects
- Historical scans
- Original work with permission

### ❌ Not Accepted:
- Commercial content
- Paywalled magazines
- Modern copyrighted books
- Leaked material

Open an issue or submit a PR with:
- Source
- Author / publisher info
- License or permission status
